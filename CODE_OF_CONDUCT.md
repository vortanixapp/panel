# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good
faith and knows something you do not.

## What we expect

- Criticise code, not people. "This drops the error" is useful; "this is
  amateur work" is not.
- Accept that maintainers will sometimes say no, and that saying no to a patch
  is not a judgement of the person who wrote it.
- Remember that most people here are not writing in their first language. Read
  past awkward phrasing rather than into it.

## What we will not accept

Harassment, insults, and personal attacks. Sexual language or imagery.
Publishing anyone's private information. Sustained disruption of discussions.
Any of these will get your contributions removed and, if repeated, get you
blocked from the project.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@vortanix.app**. Reports are read by the maintainers and are not
shared further without your agreement. If your report concerns a maintainer,
say so — it will be handled by someone else.

We will acknowledge a report within three working days and tell you what, if
anything, we did about it.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere
else someone is representing this project.
